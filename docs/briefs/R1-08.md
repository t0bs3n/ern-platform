# R1-08 Events and deadlines

## What was built
- **Events table:**
  - title, kind (Conference, Lecture, Deadline, Association), date, and an optional start and end time
  - time zone, place and an optional link
  - Times are stored in UTC and shown in the event's own time zone, with the zone named, for example "Wednesday 16 December 2026, 14:00 to 16:00 (Oslo time)". Dates without a time are deadlines that end with the day.
- **Conference dates appear automatically:** for every upcoming conference, its key dates (as deadlines, for example "ERNC27: abstract deadline") and its conference days.
- **`/events`**, as in the mockup:
  - the filter Everything, Conferences, Lectures, Deadlines, Association (plain links, no JavaScript)
  - the note about time zones
  - all entries that are not over yet, in order, with their kind
  - "Subscribe to the calendar (.ics)"
- **`/events.ics`**: an iCalendar feed of the same entries for calendar apps.
  - Timed events carry their time zone (TZID), with the zone's summer and winter time rules (VTIMEZONE).
  - Deadlines are all-day entries.
  - Each entry has a fixed ID, so calendars update instead of doubling.
- **The Content tab** has a new card, "Events and deadlines", where editors add, change and remove events. The default time zone is `Europe/Berlin` (the setting `ERN_EVENT_TIMEZONE`). Links must start with `https://` or `/`.
- **The start page's "Coming up"** now shows the next three entries of the whole calendar, as R1-02 planned.
- **Example events** for a local computer (`EventSeeder`, local and tests only, because the dates are the mockup's examples):
  - the board nominations
  - the motions
  - the General Assembly on 16 December 2026, 14:00 Oslo time
  - the ERNC27 presenter registration deadline

## How to see it
`php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, `php artisan serve`, then /events and /events.ics. Sign in as `editor@example.test` to add events under Admin area, Content.

## What was tested (19 new tests)
- **The list:**
  - it is in order and hides past entries
  - it includes the conference key dates and days
  - timed events show the time and the named zone
- **The filter:** by kind, without JavaScript. An unknown kind shows everything.
- **The .ics feed:**
  - its structure (begin and end, matching events)
  - time zones for Oslo and New York, with their offsets
  - all-day dates with the right end day
  - commas and semicolons escaped
  - every line at most 75 bytes, ending in CRLF
  - every event with an ID and a time stamp
- Entries are ordered by when they begin, also across time zones.
- Links in the feed are full addresses. Links with line breaks, spaces or backslashes, or links that lead off the site through `//`, are refused, and the feed leaves out a bad link even if one got into the database.
- A zone without summer time (Tokyo) gets its offset.
- Two key dates with the same name keep different IDs.
- Long lines with special characters are folded without breaking a character.
- **Editing:**
  - an editor adds an event (stored in UTC), changes it to all-day and removes it
  - wrong input is refused with plain messages, including a `javascript:` link and an unknown time zone
- **Access:** all five event addresses are refused for a guest (sent to sign-in), a member and the lecture team, and nothing changes.
- **The start page:** "Coming up" follows the calendar, and the conference itself is still listed after its key dates have passed.
- **By hand:** /events at 1280 and 375 pixels, with no sideways scrolling, and the feed's time-zone block.

## What was not tested
- The feed in real calendar apps (Apple, Google, Outlook). It follows the standard (RFC 5545), but a check with a real app on staging is worth doing.

## Good to know
- **"Add to calendar" for single entries:** the mockup has a button on each entry. Here, the whole calendar is subscribed to once. Single-entry files are an idea for later.
- **Time zones:** the mockup shows times "in your own time zone". `docs/DECISIONS.md` says times are "shown in the time zone of the event, with the zone named", and that decision is followed here.
- **The autumn lectures** of the mockup come with the teaching library (release 3).

- **Conference dates are in Berlin time** (`ERN_EVENT_TIMEZONE`), even for a conference in Raleigh. A deadline therefore leaves the list at midnight Berlin time.
- **Wording not in the mockup:** the box "In your own calendar. Subscribe once, and new dates appear in your calendar by themselves." Please check it, Tobi.
