# R1-02 Start page

## What was built
The start page as in the mockup (`js/10-home.js`):
- **Hero:** "Research-focused. Globally connected.", the lead text, and the buttons "Join ERN" and "ERNC27 Details".
- **Three starting points:** Find research, Teach with ERN, and Join and contribute, each with its three links.
- **Featured Event:** ERNC27 with its theme, dates, format and place, the links Call for Abstracts, Submit and Code of Conduct, and "Learn More".
- **Coming up:** the next three ERNC27 key dates that have not passed, with "All events and deadlines". The block disappears when no date is left.
- **News & Updates:** the three latest published posts, newest first, linking to `/news/{address}`, with "View all". Drafts and posts dated in the future never appear. Without any posts, the block is left out.
- **What ERN Does:** the mission, the eight goals and the ERNC26 group photo.
- **Get Involved:** Become a Member, Volunteer, Join Discord.
- The goals and the "Get Involved" cards are shared pieces (`resources/views/partials/`), so About Us and Get Involved (R1-04) can reuse them.
- The icon set now holds all 32 icons of the mockup.

## How to see it
`php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, then `php artisan serve`, then http://localhost:8000.

## What was tested
- Every block of the mockup is on the page, and there is no "For members" block (as decided).
- Only the three latest published posts show, newest first. There are no drafts and no posts dated in the future. The links point to the post addresses.
- Without news, the news block is left out.
- "Coming up" shows only key dates that have not passed, and it disappears when none is left.
- There is one h1, and nothing loads from other websites.
- **By hand:** the page at 1280 and 375 pixels wide, with no sideways scrolling.

## What was not tested
- **Links:** many lead to pages built in later tasks (news, conferences, events, join, the database and teaching), so they answer "Page not found" for now.

## Good to know
- **Where the ERNC27 details live:** the featured event and its key dates are in `config/ern.php` for now, because conferences (R1-06) and events (R1-08) are not built yet. Those tasks should switch the start page to the database and remove the setting. In the mockup, "Coming up" lists all events and deadlines (lectures, the General Assembly and others), and R1-08 must feed it from the events. The "Submit" link goes to `/conferences/ernc27/submit`. R1-06 points the submit button to the old site's form, and this link should follow.
- **Left out on purpose:** the mockup's search box in the hero. It searches the database, which comes in release 2. A search box that finds nothing would mislead visitors. Add it with release 2.
- **News links:** the mockup's news cards open the post on the old website. Here they open our own post page (R1-03).
