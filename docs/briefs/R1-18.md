# R1-18 Old addresses

## What was built
- **A redirect table** in `config/redirects.php`: one line per old address and the new one. All redirects are permanent (301), so search engines move their links over.
  - `/conference` → `/conferences`
  - `/getinvolved` → `/get-involved`
  - `/ernc21-summary`, `/ernc22-summary`, `/ernc24-summary`, `/ernc25-summary` → `/conferences/ernc21` and so on
- **Old news addresses** such as `/2026/08/10/get-involved-with-the-esports-research-network-ern-board/` lead to the post of the same name, `/news/get-involved-with-the-esports-research-network-ern-board`. This works for every old post copied over. An old address whose post is not (or not yet) public answers "Page not found".
- **The same address on both sites:** `/about`, `/get-involved`, `/news` and `/imprint` answer directly, with or without the slash at the end, and need no redirect.
- **Left out until their new page exists,** with a note in the file:
  - the concern form (`/concern/` → `/report-a-concern`): R1-17's task text now says to add it
  - `/lectures/` → `/teaching` (release 3)
  - `/submission/`, which stays on the old site until our own submission form exists (release 5)

  A redirect to a page that does not exist would only give "Page not found".

## How to see it
`php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, `php artisan serve`, then open http://localhost:8000/conference or an old news address, for example http://localhost:8000/2026/02/20/best-selling-esports-textbook-releases-second-edition-esports-business-management-2nd-edition/.

## What was tested (5 new tests)
- Each listed address, with and without the slash at the end, redirects permanently once, to a page that answers 200.
- The known addresses of the task work, either as redirects or directly.
- All ten old news addresses lead to their posts, which answer.
- An unknown old news address and the address of a draft answer 404.
- Every redirect stays on this site.

## What was not tested
- **The full list of the old site's addresses.** The task names `docs/old-site-addresses.txt`, which Tobi or a later task supplies. Only the addresses known from the mockup are covered. Once the list exists, each missing address is one line in `config/redirects.php`.
- **On the hosting:** the address with a slash at the end is handled by the framework here. On the hosting, the `.htaccess` file does the same. Check on staging.
