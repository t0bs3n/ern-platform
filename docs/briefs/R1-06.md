# R1-06 Conferences overview

## What was built
- **The conferences table**: name, title, theme, place, format, host, first and last day, status (upcoming or past), the introduction, the summary and facts of past conferences, the key dates, and links to the Book of Abstracts (PDF), the videos and the old summary page. It is seeded with ERNC27 and the five past conferences from `data.json`.
- **`/conferences`**, as in the mockup:
  - the next conference with its dates, format, place and key dates, plus "Call for Abstracts", "Submit an Abstract" and "Explore Past Conferences"
  - the theme with its introduction and three questions
  - the archive "Past ERN Conferences", newest first, each with Summary, Abstracts (PDF on the current site) and Videos
- **`/conferences/{name}`** (also `/summary`) for a past conference: the "Conference Overview", the facts, and the box "Abstracts and recordings".
- **`/conferences/ernc27`** (also `/call`):
  - the Call for Abstracts (the page `ernc27-call-for-abstracts` from R1-01), with jump links
  - the "At a glance" box with the key dates and "Submit an abstract"
- **`/conferences/ernc27/submit`** sends visitors to the submission form on the current website. The address is a setting, `ERN_SUBMISSION_URL` in `.env`, and defaults to `https://esportsresearch.net/submission/`.
- **The Conference tab** (`/admin/conference`): the conference team and admins see all conferences and edit:
  - theme, place, format and host
  - the first and last day, and the status
  - up to twelve key dates

  Reviewers and the final editor see the tab, but not the list.
- **The start page** now takes the featured event and "Coming up" from the upcoming conference. The setting from R1-02 is gone.
- Dates read like the mockup, for example "9 to 11 November 2027" or "30 October to 1 November 2024".

## How to see it
`php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, `php artisan serve`, then /conferences, /conferences/ernc27 and /conferences/ernc25. Sign in as `conference_team@example.test` to edit at /admin/conference.

## What was tested (16 new tests)
- **The overview:** the next conference, its key dates, and all five past conferences, newest first.
- **Each past conference page:** its summary.
- **The ERNC27 call:**
  - the dates, jump links and the submit button
  - "Submit" leads to the configured address, and gives 404 for a past conference
  - unknown conferences and tabs answer 404
- **The start page** follows the conference.
- **Date wording:** all four cases.
- **Editing:**
  - the conference team edits the dates and key dates, and the public pages follow
  - wrong dates are refused with plain messages
  - a guest, a member, an editor and a reviewer cannot edit
  - reviewers and the final editor see the tab without the list, and an editor is refused
  - an admin edits, and a wrong status is refused
- **By hand:** all three public pages at 1280 and 375 pixels, with no sideways scrolling, and the edit form.

## What was not tested
- Whether the links to the Books of Abstracts and the videos on the current site and YouTube still work. They are copied from the mockup.
