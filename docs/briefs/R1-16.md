# R1-16 Admin overview

## What was built
- **The Overview tab** (`/admin`), as in the mockup:
  - **"Needs attention":** one line per open queue, with its owner and a button. Today's queues:
    - membership applications that wait for the board, with "Confirm or decline" (for admins, who manage members)
    - members who asked to end their membership, with "See the member list" (admins)
    - news posts that are still drafts, and notices that are still drafts, with "Open the content" (editors and admins)

    Without any queue: "Nothing waits for you at the moment."
  - **Number tiles:** applications to confirm and members (for admins), and drafts (for editors). Each tile opens its tab.
  - **"Who sees what"**, with the mockup's wording.
  - **"Coming up":** the next four events and deadlines.
- **Each person sees only the queues and counts their roles cover.** A reviewer, for example, sees no member or content numbers. The queues live in one class (`App\Admin\Attention`), so later releases add theirs there (submissions, the database queue, teaching).

## How to see it
`php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, `php artisan serve`, sign in as `admin@example.test` or `editor@example.test` (password `ern-local-test`), and open the Admin area.

## What was tested (5 new tests)
- **An admin** sees every queue with the right counts (open applications only, not declined ones), and the tiles and "Who sees what".
- **An editor** sees only the content queues and the drafts tile, with no member numbers.
- **A reviewer** sees no queue and no numbers from other areas.
- **The counts follow the data**, including "Nothing waits for you".
- Guests are sent to sign-in, and members are refused.
- **By hand:** the page at desktop width, and at 375 pixels with no sideways scrolling.

## What was not tested
- Real phones and screen readers. That is R1-20.

## Good to know
- The mockup's other queues (submissions without reviewers, decisions not sent, lecture proposals, the database queue, teaching units) belong to releases 2 to 5. They are not shown as empty lines.
- **"Coming up" without events** is left out, while the mockup always shows the box.
- **A flaky test from R1-13 fixed:** the directory test gave the viewing member a random country, which sometimes matched the hidden member's ("Iceland") and failed. The member now has a fixed country.
- **New wording:**
  - "Nothing waits for you at the moment."
  - "… member asks to end the membership"
  - "… news post is still a draft", "… notice is still a draft"
  - the "Drafts" tile

None.
