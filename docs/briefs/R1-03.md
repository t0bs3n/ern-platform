# R1-03 News

## What was built
- **`/news`**: all published posts, newest first, twelve per page, with "Newer posts" and "Older posts" and page numbers. Below them is the mockup's box "More for members", with "Become a member" and "Sign in" (for guests), or a short note (for signed-in members). There is also a link to the RSS feed.
- **`/news/{address}`**: one post with its title, date, category, image (if any) and text. Drafts and posts dated in the future answer "Page not found", for everyone. Editors see drafts in the preview of the Content tab.
- **`/feed/news.xml`**: an RSS 2.0 feed of the ten latest published posts (title, link, date, summary, category). The news page tells feed readers where it is.
- The page title and the description for search engines come from the post.

## How to see it
`php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, then `php artisan serve`. Open http://localhost:8000/news, any post, and http://localhost:8000/feed/news.xml.

## What was tested (10 new tests)
- **The list:** only published posts, newest first. No drafts and no future posts. It shows the mockup wording and the box for members.
- **Pages:** with 14 posts, there are 12 on page 1 and 2 on page 2, with "Older posts" and "Newer posts".
- **A single post:**
  - the Markdown text, date, category and page title are shown
  - drafts, future posts and unknown addresses answer 404, also for signed-in members
- **The feed:**
  - well-formed RSS 2.0 with the content type `application/rss+xml`
  - exactly the ten latest posts, the newest first, with links and dates
  - no drafts, and characters such as `&` and `<` are escaped
- **The feed link:** the news page tells feed readers where the feed is.
- A page number past the last page answers 404. An empty list and an empty feed still work. Signed-in members see their own box. Future posts stay out of the feed.
- **By hand:** a long post title on a 375-pixel phone, with no sideways scrolling, and the feed's answer.

## What was not tested
- The feed in a real feed reader (it is checked as XML only).

## Good to know
- **"More for members":** the mockup's box for signed-in members has buttons to new articles and the press. Those come in release 6, so for now the box only names them, without buttons.
- **The old posts:** the seeded posts show only their summary, because the full texts of the old site are not in the mockup data (see R1-01).
