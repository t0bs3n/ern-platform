# R1-15 Members list and rights editor

Planned by the `planner` agent and built as planned.

## What was built
- **The member list** in the Members tab (`/admin/members`, for admins), below the applications:
  - "Member list" with "Export the list", and a search by name, country or institution. It is a plain form and works without JavaScript.
  - The count ("16 members" or "1 of 16 members").
  - The mockup's table: Name, Status, Country, Role on the platform, and "Edit rights". 50 to a page, and the search is kept when paging.
  - A member who asked to leave is marked "Asked to end the membership on …".
  - Only approved members appear. No e-mail addresses.
- **The member page** (`/admin/members/{id}`):
  - status, institution, country, member since
  - the rights box "Rights of …": each role with its name and description, Member ticked and fixed, and "Save the rights"
- **Only admins change roles.** The page checks this on the server, twice. The rules are all in one place (`App\Members\ChangeRoles`):
  - The member role always stays.
  - Nobody removes their own admin role: "You cannot remove your own admin role. Ask another admin to do it."
  - The last admin cannot lose the role: "The platform needs at least one admin. Give the role to someone else first."
  - **One person holds the final editor role**, as `docs/DECISIONS.md` says. Giving it to someone takes it from the current holder, and the message says so: "Final editor was taken from …, because one person holds this role."
  - Unknown roles are refused.
  - A removed role takes effect on the person's next page.
- **Export:**
  - A CSV file `ern-members-YYYY-MM-DD.csv`, which opens in Excel and other spreadsheet programs.
  - The columns are first name, last name, status, institution, country, member since, roles, and "end of membership requested". There is no e-mail address.
  - It holds all approved members, not only the search result.
  - Cells that a spreadsheet would run as a formula get a leading apostrophe.
  - The file is not cached, and the export works only through the button (POST), not through a pasted link.
- **The members log** (new table `member_admin_log`):
  - one line per role given or removed (who, whose, which role, when)
  - one line per export (who, when, how many rows)
  - No names and no addresses. Lines stay when people are deleted, with the person left empty.
- The directory's safe search helper is now shared (`App\Support\Like`).
- Hidden screen-reader text in scrolling tables no longer makes the page scroll sideways on phones.

## How to see it
`php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, `php artisan serve`, sign in as `admin@example.test` (password `ern-local-test`), Admin area, Members.

## What was tested (19 new tests)
- **Access:** all four addresses (list, member, roles, export) are refused for a guest (sent to sign-in), a member, an editor, the conference team and an unapproved admin. Nothing changes and nothing is logged.
- **The list:**
  - the mockup columns, and no e-mail address
  - search by name, country and institution, with wildcards harmless and the search kept when paging
  - unapproved users are not listed, and their pages answer 404
  - the end-of-membership request is shown
- **Changing roles:**
  - giving and removing roles, with one log line each, and nothing logged when nothing changes
  - the member role always stays
  - unknown roles, and a role list that is not a list, are refused
  - giving the final editor role when nobody holds it
- **Safeguards:**
  - an admin cannot remove their own admin role
  - another admin can be removed while one remains, and the last one cannot, also through the action itself
  - two admins cannot remove each other one after the other
  - the final editor moves, and both changes are logged
  - a removed role takes effect at once
- **The log:** it holds no names or addresses, and it survives the deletion of a member.
- **The export:**
  - the agreed columns, no e-mail address, no unapproved users
  - formulas made harmless
  - not cached, logged, and POST only
- **By hand:** saving rights in the browser without JavaScript, and every admin page at 375 pixels with no sideways scrolling.

## What was not tested
- **Sorting:** the list and the export sort by name, then by an internal number, so two members with the same name are never skipped or doubled.
- **Two admins at the very same moment:** the locks that stop two admins from removing each other then are enforced by MySQL only. The test database ignores them.
- **Real spreadsheet programs:** the file was checked as text, not opened in Excel.
