# R1-10 Approving members

Planned by the `planner` agent and built as planned.

## What was built
- **The Members tab** (`/admin/members`, for admins, who hold the "manage members" right):
  - "Applications to confirm": each open application, newest first, with "Applied on …", the name, career stage, institution and country, and the buttons "Approve" and "Decline". These are plain forms and work without JavaScript.
  - Decisions of the last 30 days, with the mockup's tags "Approved, welcome e-mail sent" and "Declined".
- **Approve:**
  - Creates the member, approved and with the member role, from the application: name, e-mail address, institution, country, career stage, interests and Discord name.
  - The member gets an address name from their name (for example `tomas-herrera`), avoiding the reserved addresses.
  - No password yet. Not listed in the directory until they choose to be.
  - Logs the decision, deletes the application, and sends the welcome e-mail.
- **The welcome e-mail** ("Welcome to the Esports Research Network"):
  - It holds the first name and a signed link to set a password, which works for 7 days.
  - The link is tied to this member and is refused if changed or expired.
  - The page behind it is a placeholder: R1-11 builds the form to set the password.
- **Decline:** marks the application as declined, logs the decision, and sends the e-mail "Your application to the Esports Research Network". It gives no reason and does not say who decided.
- **Deleting declined applications:** a job (`ern:delete-declined-applications`) runs every night and deletes each declined application 30 days after the decision.
- **The decision log** (`membership_decisions`) keeps:
  - which application, approved or declined, by whom and when
  - the date of the application
  - for approvals, the new member and the time they agreed to the Code of Conduct and the privacy policy

  It also notes whether the e-mail to the applicant went out. It holds no name, no e-mail address and no other detail of the applicant, so it stays after the deletion. If the deciding admin's account is later deleted, the entry stays without their name.
- **Safeguards:**
  - When two admins click at the same time, only one decision counts. The other sees "This application was already decided.", and no second e-mail is sent.
  - An address that already belongs to a user is refused, and the application stays open.
  - If the welcome e-mail fails, the approval stands and the admin is told that the new member can use "Forgot your password?".
- **Example applicants** for a local computer (`ApplicationSeeder`): the three invented applicants of the mockup.

## How to see it
`php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, `php artisan serve`, sign in as `admin@example.test` (password `ern-local-test`), open Admin area, Members. The e-mails are written to `storage/logs/laravel.log`.

## What was tested (29 new tests)
- **Access:** the tab, approving and declining are refused for a guest (sent to sign-in), a member, an editor and the conference team, and for an admin who is not approved. Nothing changes and nothing is sent.
- **The tab:** it shows the mockup wording, and the empty list says "No applications wait for the board."
- **Approving:**
  - it creates an approved member with only the member role
  - it copies interests, Discord name and career stage, sets no password, and leaves the member unlisted
  - it deletes the application
  - it logs without any personal data
  - the welcome e-mail goes to the applicant only, with no copy to anyone
- **Approval safeguards:**
  - an address that belongs to a user is refused
  - deciding twice creates one member and sends one e-mail
  - a failing e-mail keeps the approval and says so, and the tag then reads "Approved, welcome e-mail not sent"
  - a failing decline e-mail keeps the decision and says so
  - e-mail errors never write the address into the log (only the kind of error is logged; the same fix was made in the application form from R1-09)
- **Declining:** it marks the application, logs, and sends the answer. The declined person can apply again.
- **The "Decided" list** shows only the last 30 days.
- **The log:** it survives the deletion of the application and of the deciding admin.
- **Addresses:**
  - an unknown application answers 404
  - member address names: accents, reserved words, names already taken, and names without Latin letters (they become `member`)
- **The link:** it works, it is refused when changed or after 7 days, and it is refused for another member.
- **The deletion job:** declined applications go after 30 days, not before. Open applications stay. The job runs every night.
- **The e-mails:** both versions. The welcome e-mail holds no details, and the decline e-mail has no reason and escapes names.
- **By hand:** approving and declining in the browser.

## What was not tested
- Real e-mail delivery.
- **Two admins at the very same moment:** this relies on a database lock that only MySQL enforces. The test database (SQLite) ignores it, so only one-after-the-other clicks were tested.
- That the hosting's scheduler runs the nightly job. R0-11 proves the scheduler runs.

## For Tobi: please decide
- **The wording of the decline e-mail** is new. The mockup has none. "Hello …, thank you for your interest in the Esports Research Network. The board has decided not to confirm your application. We delete your application 30 days from now. If you have questions, write to board@esportsresearch.net."
- **Other new wording:**
  - "No applications wait for the board."
  - "This application was already decided."
  - "This e-mail address already belongs to a member. The application stays open."
  - the welcome e-mail's last lines ("The link works for 7 days and only once…")
- **No "Undo":** the mockup has an Undo button. Once the e-mail has gone, a decision cannot be called back, so decisions are final here. A wrong approval can be withdrawn later in the members list (R1-15). Someone declined by mistake can apply again.
- **No confirmation step:** the buttons act at once, as in the mockup. Should there be an "Are you sure?" step?
- **The city is not kept:** members have no city field, and neither the profile nor the directory in the mockup shows one. It disappears with the application. The privacy policy should say so.

## Good to know (for later tasks)
- **R1-11:**
  - The link points to `/members/activate/{member}` (route name `members.activate`).
  - It accepts the link only while the member has no password, so the link works once.
  - It makes the expired-link page friendly, and it sends a new link with `App\Members\ActivationLink::for($member)`.
  - "Forgot your password?" also works for a new member.
- **R1-12 or R1-15**, whichever withdraws an approval or ends a membership, must also sign the person out by deleting their sessions.
- Reserved member address names are in `User::RESERVED_SLUGS`.
