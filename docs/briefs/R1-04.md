# R1-04 About Us and Get Involved

## What was built
- **`/about`**, as in the mockup:
  - the jump links "On this page"
  - Our Goals
  - Members: figures, the member map, the largest groups and "As of"
  - the Mission Statement with a photo
  - Board Members, and Governance & Volunteers, as cards with initials
  - Our History, with the former board members
- **`/get-involved`**, as in the mockup:
  - the three "Get Involved" cards
  - "A Community-Led Network" with a photo
  - the ten Ways to Participate
  - "How to Get Started" with "Become a Member", "Join Discord" and the board's address
- **Content editors change:**
  - **The board and the volunteers** (new table `team_members`), in a new "Board and volunteers" card in the Content tab: add a person, edit name, institution, role, group and order, or remove them. Removing asks for a separate button press, on a separate form below.
  - **The texts** of the mission, the history, the former board members, the community text and "How to Get Started". They are pages (`about-mission`, `about-history`, `about-former-board`, `get-involved-community`, `get-involved-start`) and are edited in the Pages list of the Content tab.
- Everything is seeded from `data.json` by `ContentSeeder`, which may run on staging and live and never overwrites edits.
- **No election candidates anywhere.** The person form says so, and it offers only the two groups "Board Members" and "Governance & Volunteers".
- The jump links are now plain links that work without JavaScript (the shared component `x-subnav`).

## How to see it
`php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, `php artisan serve`, then http://localhost:8000/about and /get-involved. Sign in as `editor@example.test` to change the board under Admin area, Content.

## What was tested (15 new tests)
- **The pages:**
  - About Us and Get Involved show all seeded blocks with the mockup wording
  - the word "candidate" does not appear
  - both pages work without seeded content
- **Editing:**
  - the board is shown in the chosen order
  - an editor's change to a text block appears on the page
  - an editor adds, moves and removes a person
  - required fields and the two allowed groups are checked
  - an empty order keeps the person's place
  - the Ombudsperson is not named
- **Access:** all five person addresses are refused for a guest (sent to sign-in), a member and the conference team, and nothing changes.
- **By hand:** both pages at 1280 and 375 pixels, with no sideways scrolling.

## What was not tested
- Real phones. That is the accessibility pass, R1-20.

## Good to know
- **The member figures** (683 members, 70 countries and so on, "As of 31 October 2026") and the map are fixed, from the mockup, in `config/ern.php` and `public/img/members.svg`. The mockup says they should come from the member list. That is possible after the member import (R1-19), and is proposed as a follow-up task. Until then, Tobi changes the numbers in `config/ern.php` by asking Claude.
- **The board and the volunteers** are real, public people from the current website.
- **The five text blocks** appear in the Pages list with their internal name, for example `about-mission`, instead of an address.

- **The Ombudsperson is not named.** The mockup lists the Ombudsperson by name under Governance & Volunteers, but `CLAUDE.md` says "Ombudsperson is the role. Do not name the person." The rule wins, so the entry is left out of the seeded content. Tobi, if the board wants the role shown, an editor can add it, but without a person's name. The rule may also need changing.
- "Become a Member" links to `/join`, which comes with R1-09. Until then it answers "Page not found".
