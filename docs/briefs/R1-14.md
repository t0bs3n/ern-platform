# R1-14 Member overview

## What was built
- **`/members`**, the member's own start page, as in the mockup's member overview:
  - **The header:** initials, "Welcome back, Maja", career stage and institution, and "Sign out".
  - **The tabs:** Overview, Directory, My profile.
  - **Notices:** the published notices for members, newest first.
  - **Coming up:** the next three events and deadlines, with "All events and deadlines".
  - **From the ERN:** the three latest news posts, with "All news".
  - **Your membership:** member since, status, listed as, directory visible or hidden, Discord name, and "Update my details".
  - **Your roles:** each role with its description. People with a role beyond member get an "Admin area" button in the header, as in the mockup.
- **Notices for members** (new table `notices`):
  - Each notice has a label (for example "General Assembly"), a title, a text in Markdown, and draft or published.
  - Editors write, publish and remove them in a new "Notices for members" card in the Content tab.
  - Notices appear only in the member area, never on public pages.
- Only approved members open the page. Guests are sent to sign-in, and a user whose approval was withdrawn is refused.
- **Left out on purpose:** the mockup's blocks for saved items, the agenda, conference submissions, new articles and the press, and the Discord bot card. They come with later releases, and the task says not to show empty shells. Blocks without content (no notices, no events) are left out too.
- Four example notices from the mockup for a local computer (`NoticeSeeder`, local and tests only).

## How to see it
`php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, `php artisan serve`, sign in as `maja.lindqvist@example.test` (password `ern-local-test`).

## What was tested (15 new tests)
- **The member's own data:** status, institution, member since, directory status, Discord name and roles, and nothing about other members. There is no admin link for a plain member.
- **Notices:** only published ones show, with their Markdown text.
- **Events:** the next events show, and empty blocks are left out.
- **Admin roles:** an editor sees their role with its description, and the admin link.
- **Access:**
  - guests are sent to sign-in, and unapproved users are refused
  - all five notice addresses are refused for a guest, a member and a Database Lead, and nothing changes
- **Editing notices:**
  - an editor writes a draft, publishes it, members see it, and the editor removes it
  - notice input is checked (missing fields, an unknown status, images from other websites)
  - notices never reach the public start page or news list
  - HTML in a notice is shown as text
  - setting a notice back to draft hides it
- **By hand:** the page at desktop width, and at 375 pixels with no sideways scrolling.

## What was not tested
- Real phones and screen readers. That is R1-20.

## Good to know
- **Documents on notices:** the mockup shows documents on notices ("Invitation", "Agenda" and so on). Uploading documents for members needs private file storage with a members-only check. It is proposed as its own small task.
- **The mockup's "Notices" tab** is folded into the overview, because the notices are few.

## Ideas left out
- Documents attached to notices.
- A separate notices page.
