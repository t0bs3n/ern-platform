# R1-11 Activation

Planned by the `planner` agent and built as planned.

## What was built
- **The link in the welcome e-mail** (`/members/activate/{member}?expires=…&signature=…`) now opens the mockup's form:
  - "Welcome to ERN", "The board has confirmed your membership. Choose a password to open the member area."
  - "New password" (with "At least eight characters."), "Repeat the password"
  - "Set password and sign in", and "I already have a password" (to sign-in)
- **After setting the password:**
  - The member is signed in with a fresh session and lands in the member area with "Your account is active. Welcome to the member area."
  - The password is stored hashed.
  - The address counts as confirmed, because the link proves it.
- **The link is checked by the page itself**, so every case gets a friendly page:
  - **Expired** (after 7 days): "This link has expired", with "Send a new link".
  - **Already used** (the member has a password): "This link was already used", with "Sign in" and "Forgot your password?".
  - **Broken, changed, or for a person who is not or no longer approved:** "This link is not valid", with no new link offered.
  - A form opened just before the link expires and sent just after it is refused too.
  - Two quick sends give one activation. The second gets "already used".
- **"Send a new link":**
  - It only works from a correctly signed, expired link. Nobody types an address, so a stranger cannot aim the e-mail at anyone.
  - It sends a fresh welcome e-mail only to the member of that link, and only while they still have no password and are approved.
  - It sends at most three a day per member, and allows ten requests an hour per internet address.
  - The answer is always the same "Check your e-mail" page.
- **Other protections:** signed-in members are sent away from these pages, and the pages accept ten requests a minute per internet address.
- The member area now shows its messages. The placeholder page from R1-10 is gone.

## How to see it
`php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, `php artisan serve`, sign in as `admin@example.test`, approve an applicant in Admin area, Members, sign out, and open the link from the welcome e-mail in `storage/logs/laravel.log`.

## What was tested (18 new tests)
- **The form:** the mockup wording, and that it posts back to the signed link.
- **A successful activation:**
  - the member is signed in and lands in the member area with the message
  - the password is hashed, and the address counts as confirmed
  - the session is renewed
- **Wrong passwords:** short and differing ones are refused with the mockup's messages, and nothing changes.
- **Problem links:**
  - an expired link shows the friendly page and sets nothing
  - a form sent after expiry is refused
  - a used link changes nothing
  - broken, changed, unsigned and unapproved links, and a deleted member's link, all show "not valid" without a new-link button
- **Signed-in members** are sent away, and nothing changes.
- **New links:**
  - the new link goes to the member only and works, while the old one stays dead
  - no new link once a password is set or the approval is withdrawn, with a byte-identical answer
  - a made-up or borrowed signature is refused
  - a member gets three new links a day at most, with the same answer every time
- The activation step refuses a member who already has a password, and keeps the password.
- An approval withdrawn between opening the form and sending it gives "not valid".
- New-link requests from one internet address are limited across members (ten an hour).
- "Forgot your password?" still works for a member without a password.
- **By hand, with JavaScript off:** the form, and the sign-in after setting the password.
- The R1-10 test of the link now expects the friendly pages (403 for broken, 410 for expired) instead of the bare refusal.

## What was not tested
- Real e-mail delivery.
- Two sends at the very same moment: the lock that prevents a double activation is enforced by MySQL only. The test database ignores it.
- **The hosting's address setup:** if the site is reached through a different address than `APP_URL`, or through a proxy that changes `https` to `http`, links could look broken. Check once on staging.
