# R1-07 Podcast and Live

## What was built
- **`/podcast`**, as in the mockup:
  - "Podcast: The Esports Research Report" with its introduction, which editors change in the Content tab (page `podcast-intro`)
  - how to take part
  - the box with host, producer and sponsor
  - the five episodes, in the mockup's order (episode 1 first). Each links to its video on YouTube, with "All episodes on YouTube".
  - No YouTube player and no YouTube images are loaded.
- **`/live`**, as in the mockup:
  - "ERN Live" with a player box
  - the "Recordings" box with the three ERNC26 keynotes and the link to the ERNC26 page
  - "Good to know"
  - The player box offers "Open the channel on Twitch" (a plain link). With JavaScript, it also offers "Start the player here", which loads the Twitch player into the page only after that click. The box says that Twitch sets its own cookies once started.
  - An optional "Next stream" line comes from the setting `ERN_LIVE_NEXT` in `.env`, for example `ERN Lecture Series, Wednesday 8 September 2027, 16:00 (Oslo time)`.
- **The podcast data** (people, contact, playlist, episodes) and **the live data** (channel, recordings) are in `config/ern.php`.

## How to see it
`php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, `php artisan serve`, then /podcast and /live.

## What was tested (7 new tests)
- **The podcast:** its text, people, all episodes in order with their YouTube links, and the playlist. It also works without its introduction.
- **The live page:**
  - the Twitch link, the start button (hidden until the script shows it), the recordings, and "Good to know"
  - the "Next stream" line when it is set, and none when it is not
- **Both pages:** no frame, no external script and no external image or media in the first response, and no Twitch or YouTube player address.
- **By hand, in a browser:**
  - the live page made no request to another website before the click
  - after the click, the Twitch player was put in place
  - both pages at 1280 and 375 pixels, with no sideways scrolling

## What was not tested
- Whether Twitch accepts the player on our domain. That needs the staging site (R0-11).
- **Whether the channel is live.** The page cannot know that yet. Showing "Live" and a banner on every page, as the mockup does, needs Twitch's interface with a key. Proposed for later.

## Good to know
- **Adding a podcast episode** or changing the next stream means changing `config/ern.php` or `.env` for now. An editor screen for episodes is an idea for later.
- **The programme:** the mockup's live programme is an ERNC26 example. The real programme of a stream comes with events (R1-08) and the conference programme (release 4), so this page has no programme box yet. The hero reads "Our Twitch channel on the site." until then. The mockup adds "with the programme next to it". The player box's heading is "Watch ERN Live", because the page cannot know whether the channel is offline.
- **No chat yet:** the chat in the mockup appears only while the channel is live, which the page cannot tell yet (see above).

## Ideas left out
- Live status from Twitch, a banner on every page while live, and the chat.
- Editing podcast episodes in the admin area.
