# R1-13 Directory and researcher pages

Planned by the `planner` agent and built as planned.

## What was built
- **`/members/directory`**, for approved members only:
  - The search ("Search by name, country or research topic") and the filters Topic, Country, Career stage and "Open to". It is a plain form and works without JavaScript.
  - The count ("16 members", or "6 of 16 members" when filtered) with "Clear search and filters".
  - Member cards with initials, name, career stage and country, interest tags, "Open to: Collaboration" and the Discord name (only if released). 24 to a page, and the filters are kept when paging.
  - "No member found. Try a country or a topic, for example Japan or governance."
- **`/members/{name}`, the researcher page:**
  - name, career stage and country, institution
  - research interests, "Open to", the ORCID iD (a link to orcid.org)
  - "On Discord" (only if released)
  - the form "Write to …" with "Send through ERN"

  On their own page, a member sees "This is your page as other members see it." with "Update my details" instead of the form.
- **Only members who chose to be listed appear,** and only if approved. This holds in the list, the search, the counts and the filter choices.
  - A hidden member's page answers exactly like an unknown address (the same 404 page), also for the member themselves.
  - Guests are sent to sign-in whatever address they try, so they learn nothing.
- **The e-mail address is never shown.** The pages do not even read it from the database.
- **Writing to a member:**
  - The message is sent at once by e-mail from the platform's address to the member only. "Reply" goes to the sender, as the note under the form says: "Your address is shown, theirs stays private until they answer."
  - The message is stored nowhere: not in the database, not in the session after an error, and not in the queue or the logs.
  - **Limits:** five messages an hour and twenty a day per sender, and three a day to the same member.
  - Nobody can write to themselves, to a hidden or unapproved member, or to an unknown address.
  - If sending fails, the page says "Your message could not be sent. Please try again later." and keeps the text in the box.
- **Search safety:** the search treats `%`, `_` and `!` as plain characters, not as wildcards. The search words must all match.
- **Fixed addresses still work:** `/members/profile`, `/members/sign-in` and the others are never mistaken for a member's page.
- The member area links to the directory. The example members now include some who are open to collaboration and some with a visible Discord name.

## How to see it
`php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, `php artisan serve`, sign in as `maja.lindqvist@example.test` (password `ern-local-test`), open the member area, then "Member directory".

## What was tested (21 new tests)
- **Access:**
  - guests are sent to sign-in alike for known, hidden and unknown addresses
  - unapproved users are refused everything
- **The list:**
  - it shows the mockup wording and only listed members
  - no hidden member's name, country or topic appears, and no e-mail address or unreleased Discord name
  - an unapproved listed user appears nowhere
- **Search:**
  - by name, several words, topic, country, career stage and "open to"
  - unknown filter values are ignored
  - wildcards find nothing extra
  - the filter choices never show a hidden member
  - pages keep the filters
- **The researcher page:**
  - released fields only, never the e-mail address
  - Discord only when released
  - a hidden member answers byte-identically to an unknown address
  - fixed member addresses still work
  - no form on the own page, and writing to oneself is refused
- **Messages:**
  - one e-mail, to the member only, with replies to the sender, sent at once and not queued
  - the text is stored nowhere: no job, nothing in the sessions or in the kept form input
  - hidden, unapproved and unknown members cannot be written to
  - an empty and an overlong message are refused with the mockup wording
  - the limits per pair, per sender per hour, and per sender per day
  - searching by an e-mail address or a hidden Discord name finds nothing
  - a failed send keeps the text and logs only the kind of error
- **The e-mail:** HTML is escaped, and it links the sender's page only if the sender is listed.
- **By hand, with JavaScript off:** filtering at desktop width, and the researcher page at 375 pixels with no sideways scrolling.

## What was not tested
- **Accents in search:** on the test database (SQLite) "chloe" does not find "Chloé". MySQL usually does.
- Real e-mail delivery, and how mail programs show the reply address.

## For Tobi to decide or check

- **Who can be written to:** the form is shown for every listed member, as in the mockup, even if they are not open to collaboration. Should it be shown only to members who are?
- **"Open to"** is free text in the mockup. The platform has one yes or no box, so the filter offers "All" and "Collaboration".
- **New wording:**
  - the "Search" button
  - the counts
  - "This is your page as other members see it."
  - "Your message is sent. … gets it by e-mail and can answer you directly."
  - "Your message could not be sent. Please try again later."
  - "Write a few words first." (from the mockup)
  - the e-mail subject "… wrote to you through ERN" and its last lines
- **More on the researcher page than in the mockup:** it adds the institution and the ORCID iD, which the profile (R1-12) promised members are shown. The mockup's "Publications, abstracts and talks" is left out until the database exists (release 2).
