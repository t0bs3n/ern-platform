The Esports Research Network (ERN) is committed to advancing esports research through global collaboration, rigorous scholarship, and meaningful connections between academia and industry.

We strive to create an inclusive, diverse, and supportive community that empowers researchers at all stages of their careers, from emerging scholars to established experts. Our mission is to bridge the gap between research and practice, ensuring that academic insights translate into real-world impact.

Through our conferences, publications, and community initiatives, we aim to tackle the key challenges facing esports and the digital society, driving innovation through interdisciplinary collaboration and fostering the next generation of esports researchers.
