The Esports Research Network (ERN) was founded to address the growing need for a centralized platform connecting researchers, practitioners, and industry professionals in the rapidly evolving field of esports research.

Since our inception, we have grown into a global community of scholars dedicated to advancing esports research through collaboration, education, and innovation. Our annual conferences have brought together hundreds of researchers from around the world, fostering interdisciplinary dialogue and driving the field forward.

Today, ERN continues to serve as a vital hub for esports research, providing resources, networking opportunities, and a platform for sharing cutting-edge research that shapes the future of esports and digital society.
